# IVX Advanced Intelligence Lab — 5 Hour Daily Program

Status: SYNCED / OWNER DIRECTIVE
Date: 2026-08-26

## Mission
Reserve a five-hour daily research and engineering window for measurable improvements to IVX Autonomous while a separate Caretaker/Watchdog protects production.

## Caretaker duties
- Monitor CI and regressions.
- Monitor exact-SHA production parity and health.
- Monitor 112-agent fleet health and certification evidence.
- Preserve Owner Gates.
- Keep experimental work isolated from production.

## Intelligence Lab tracks
1. Planning and decomposition.
2. Operational memory and remedy memory.
3. Self-evaluation and evidence quality.
4. Tool selection and orchestration.
5. Multi-agent coordination.
6. Causal/root-cause diagnosis.
7. Simulation and adversarial/chaos testing.
8. Safe self-improvement with benchmark comparison.
9. Quantum-inspired optimization research.
10. Quantum-computing research when real hardware/provider evidence exists.

AGI and quantum intelligence are research goals, not claims of achieved capability. Quantum-inspired algorithms must never be represented as execution on quantum hardware.

## 30-minute yoga / recovery interval
During each five-hour session include one 30-minute no-change cooldown. Experimental mutations pause; the Caretaker continues monitoring production. Use the interval to consolidate logs, observations and benchmark state before resuming experimentation.

## Production safety
Experiments remain on isolated branches/sandboxes. No experiment may weaken tests, evidence, security, 112/112 fail-closed certification, or Owner Gates. Production changes require normal CI, verification, exact-SHA evidence and applicable owner approval.

## Daily evidence
Every session ends with:
- before/after benchmark;
- experiments performed;
- measurable gains or regressions;
- failures and lessons;
- artifacts/commit SHAs when applicable;
- safety/Owner-Gate events;
- next experiment.

## Success principle
Credit is earned for measurable, reproducible capability improvement, not for labels such as AGI, superintelligence or quantum intelligence.
