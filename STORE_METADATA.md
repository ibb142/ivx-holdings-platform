### Description
IVX Holdings is a fractional real estate investment platform that makes property ownership accessible to everyone. No accreditation required, no minimum income threshold — just verify your identity and start building your portfolio.

**KEY FEATURES:**

• Fractional Property Ownership — Buy shares in premium real estate properties starting at just $1 per share. Own a piece of luxury condos, commercial buildings, and development projects.

• Rental Dividends — Earn quarterly distributions from rental income generated by your properties. Track earnings in real-time through your portfolio dashboard.

• Secondary Marketplace — Trade your property shares with other investors. List your shares for sale, browse buy orders, and execute trades securely within the app.

• AI Investment Assistant — Chat with our AI-powered assistant to analyze deals, understand risk factors, and get answers to your investment questions.

• Portfolio Analytics — Track your total returns, dividend yield, property performance, and allocation across asset classes with comprehensive analytics tools.

• JV Partnerships — Browse and participate in Joint Venture real estate deals. Partner with other investors on larger acquisition opportunities.

• Secure KYC Verification — Complete identity verification directly in the app. Government ID upload, biometric matching, and sanctions screening — all encrypted and secure.

• Deal Marketplace — Browse active investment opportunities with full disclosure documents, property details, financial projections, and risk factors.

• Owner Dashboard — For property owners and admins: manage deals, review applications, broadcast updates, and monitor platform health.

**WHO IS IVX FOR?**

IVX Holdings is for anyone who wants to invest in real estate but doesn't have hundreds of thousands of dollars to buy a whole property. Whether you're a first-time investor or building a diversified portfolio, IVX makes real estate investing as easy as buying a product online.
